## Shared semantic dictionary and component matrix

Let S_t be the set of values at stream indices 0,...,t−1. Lean histories have length t and keep order; empirical probabilities divide the number of distinct S_t members in a group by |S_t|. Repetition changes history/time but not weights. This matches the source's x_1,...,x_t after shifting stream indices by one. A law has a nonnegative real mass function, summable over the ambient type, with total exactly one. `SupportedOn` excludes nonzero mass outside a set; it does not require full support. Probability one on a fresh target set and this support-inclusion predicate agree for these discrete laws; the native interface includes that equivalence.

Write R_a(G) for global coordinate error at most a on every stream at each t>0, and C(G,L,s,t) for unit mass on L minus S_t. Then the actual predicates are:

- U_a: exists G,d with R_a(G), and for every L in H, every stream contained in L, and every t with |S_t|=d, C holds at every later time.
- NU: for every a>0, exists G with R_a(G), and for each L in H exists d before quantifying over valid streams and trigger times.
- LIM: for every a>0, exists G with R_a(G), and for every L in H and every presentation of all L, exists T before all t≥T consistency obligations.

No PAC confidence parameter, input sampling distribution, independence assumption, joint output process, running-time rate, or effective selection is hidden here. Witnesses G may vary with a. Limit T may depend on the full presentation; uniform d cannot. H may be empty unless separately excluded.
