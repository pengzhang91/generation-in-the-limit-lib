## C04 — explicit undecidable support membership

**Source versus Lean.** Appendix A, printed 91 / PDF 94, proposes one static input-free machine. Proposition A.1 asserts undecidable support membership. No learning distribution, rate, family nontriviality or identifiability premise applies. The frozen native census has PMF/MOP interfaces and positive random-machine support results, but no matching explicit encoded witness or halting reduction. The registry/PaperMap acknowledge that exact omission; they supply no hidden endpoint.

**Smallest branch repair.** Step 2 defines b as encoding validity and step 3 visibly tests r=1. Replacing that occurrence by b=1 is the smallest coherent reading. Under an effective injective machine/input encoding, distinguishable tagged outputs and an independent uniform ternary tape, a length-m encoded prefix followed by delimiter has probability 3^(-(m+1))>0. The repaired machine emits the associated tagged output iff that encoded machine halts. An assumed support decider would therefore decide arbitrary halting instances. This is a local informal argument, not Lean-checked and not certification of the paper's full proof. The literal r-test does not express this reduction and remains non-equivalent.

**Separate probability-model issue.** Fix an encoded infinite-loop machine. Its finite prefix has positive probability, and on that event the repaired generator never outputs. Hence the unconditioned output law has mass strictly less than one; the b-repair alone does not make a `PMF Nat`. A partial finite-output/support interpretation validates the reduction. If a normalized almost-surely terminating sampler is required, one can additionally randomize a finite simulation horizon with full support and emit the tagged result only on bounded halting, using fallback otherwise. That is a different construction, not a claimed literal formalization or a minimal typo repair. No such undecidability witness is supplied in Lean here. PaperMap's distinction between nonterminating outer execution and a normalized PMF (lines 411--425) is relevant model context, not a formalization of A.1.

**Disposition and follow-up.** Real missing formalization, with a justified source branch correction and an unresolved/qualified output-law interpretation. No Lean statement mistranslation has been demonstrated because no matching theorem is present.
