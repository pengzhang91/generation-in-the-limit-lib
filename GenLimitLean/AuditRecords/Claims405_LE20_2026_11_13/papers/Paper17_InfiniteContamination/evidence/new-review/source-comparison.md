## Independent source assertion and standing assumptions

For a fixed collection of infinite languages, target K, input enumeration x_1,x_2,... and supplied set-based generator, let A_n be its infinite output and L_n its finite subcollection. For every n, A_n is contained in the intersection of L_n minus the first n input elements. Eventually K belongs to L_n and mu_low(A_n,K)>=rho. The surrounding theorem assumes generation in the limit; the eventual target-inclusion and containment hypotheses already imply eventual set validity. The proof separates the trivial rho=0 case and proves Claim 6.17 for **rho>0**, with rho in [0,1] in the setup.

The source's maintained m_n is first the least positive simultaneous density-rate threshold for A_n against all L in L_n, using slack 1+2^(-n); it is then increased to max(m_n,m_(n-1)+1). At round n, k(n) is the greatest k in {1,...,n} satisfying m_k/(1+2^(-k))<=2*n/rho, with fallback 1. Algorithm 8 outputs the least element of A_(k(n)) outside the n observed inputs and n-1 earlier outputs.

Claim 6.17 asserts `exists n_star, forall n>=n_star, w_n in first_B(n)(K)`, where

`B(n) = ceil(2*n*(1+2^(-k(n)))/rho)`.

This prefix membership includes target membership. The threshold may depend on the fixed target, enumeration, generator data, and rho; there is no uniform rate. The paper uses increasing canonical order, one-based target ranks, and ordinarily distinct input observations. Contamination in this conditional conversion has no required finite/vanishing/constant rate and no bounded-displacement assumption. Density is the liminf of target-prefix ratios, not an input-order frequency, loss, or probability. Section 3.7 allows collection queries, including density and density-rate oracles, while prohibiting queries to the unknown target as target or future input. Algorithm 8 knows rho. These standing facts are part of C03, not separate claim audits.

## Target dossiers and dependencies

### Original rank endpoint

`GenLimit.InfiniteContamination.claim_6_17_algorithmEight_rank`, exported anchor `GenLimit.Paper17_InfiniteContamination.AlgorithmEight:756`, was rechecked in both direct target exports. With fixed setGen, its all-history infinitude certificate, family, selection, ordered family and rho>0, it assumes the global core-minus-sample invariant; carrier equality for every family index; strictly increasing order enumerations; eventual selection of the fixed target along the fixed stream; and eventual **pointwise-in-time** lowerDensity(A_t)>=rho. It concludes an existential threshold followed by the natural rank inequality at every later t for the named generator `algorithmEightGenerator setGen hinfinite selection orders rho`.

Write g for that generator, s_t for `algorithmEightIndexOn ... stream t`, and delta_s=1+(1/2)^(s+1). Its bound is exactly

`canonicalRank K K.carrier (output(g,stream,t)) <= ceil_Nat(2*(t+1)*delta_(s_t)/rho)`.

Data/g are fixed before target and stream; selection and density thresholds come after target/stream; the rank threshold is further downstream. The generator's data do not include target, future input, noise labels, or the eventual thresholds. This is eventual-for-all-time, deterministic and conditional, without an explicit rate, a uniform stabilization bound, or an element-density conclusion. No rho<=1 premise is present; rho>1 makes its eventual density premise unsatisfiable since prefix ratios are bounded by1. The rho=0 case is excluded, matching the nontrivial source branch.

`canonicalRank K K.carrier x` is zero for x outside K; for a member it counts the target carrier through canonicalIndex+1 and so is its one-based rank. Hence the rank endpoint alone cannot be called the full source prefix-membership statement.

### Quantifiers, representation and boundary checks

The source fixes a run and has `exists n_star, forall n>=n_star`; both Lean statements preserve that order for their own streams. The Lean named generator is fixed across all targets/streams satisfying its hypotheses. It has finite ordered histories `(Fin t -> Nat)`, finite selections, and semantic sets. Different indices may name the same language; finite intersections deduplicate equal language values, while the cutoff maximum still runs over selected indices. All order carriers are infinite by injective enumeration onto the carrier, and carrier equality makes all family languages infinite in the rank theorem.

No stream injectivity, target-coverage, noise-rate, omission-rate, or displacement premise occurs in either endpoint. This broadens the conditional input model; it does not assert success on every contaminated stream without the eventual selection/density premises. Empty selection is allowed early, with universal core and cutoff default0; eventual target selection excludes it eventually. Global infinitude prevents exhausted chosen sets, including on off-run histories. These global conditions should not be silently replaced by pathwise assumptions.

Source n is the number of observations at round n. Lean t is also its history length, with observations indexed below t, and an additional output exists at t=0. Under the history-preserving identification n=t and source k=s, the factors and slack in A2 differ. Under n=t+1,k=s+1 the arithmetic agrees but selected original set outputs have s rather than s+1 observations and the current history has t rather than t+1. Thus no single documented relabeling has been established for the complete procedure. The changed threshold rule is independently concrete even if a time-shift convention later resolves the arithmetic. These differences may change pointwise cutoffs in either direction; no unconditional stronger/weaker comparison or rate-equivalence claim is justified.
