## C03: source correction with all premises checked

Source Claim 4.11 (printed 38/PDF42) states eventual rank drop for a sequence with K as its limit point; its proof uses actual convergence. The owner explains that proper/distinct terms were omitted. A constant sequence shows a problem only if limit point means a sequence limit; it is not an accumulation point of its singleton range. A stronger informal counterexample avoids that terminology shortcut.

Let K=N and let L_q be N minus {q}, with q running through the positive naturals, and X={K}∪{L_q:q>=1}. X is countable and every member is infinite. In the source basis U_(L,F)={Lprime:F⊆Lprime⊆L}, each L_q is isolated because no distinct member of X is a subset of L_q. Every finite F⊆K is contained in L_q for all sufficiently large q, so K is a genuine accumulation point. Consequently X^(1)={K} and X^(2)=∅, using the source ambient derivatives or the Lean internal derivatives. The zero-based point levels are 0 for L_q and 1 for K; a uniform one-based shift would not change the failure below.

For n>=0 take J_(2n)=L_(n+1) and J_(2n+1)=K. This sequence actually converges to K in the source topology, has K as an accumulation point of its range, satisfies the finite-class-rank premise, and has all terms contained in K. It fails the distinctness/properness premise precisely at its infinitely many K terms. Those terms have rank equal to K, so the claimed eventual strict inequality fails. Thus this is a counterexample to the unqualified sentence, not to either qualified Lean theorem.

The smallest relevant repair is actual convergence plus eventual avoidance of K, within the finite-rank setting. `claim_4_11` explicitly assumes basis convergence and ∀n,J_n≠K; the registered `claim_4_11_of_convergentProperTower` assumes every J_n⊊K and eventual containment of each terminal element. Eventual exceptions can be removed by taking a tail, informally. For an exact-rank-r terminal, K is isolated in D_r; a finite neighborhood contains no other D_r point. Convergence and terminal exclusion eventually put J_n outside D_r, and finite derivative levels give its smaller exact rank. This validates the local repair informally. It does not validate Lemma 4.12, the static-tree definition or the missing Theorem 4.5 algorithm/proof. The inconsistent rank numbering on printed 35/PDF39 remains separately recorded.

**C11 literal identification (printed 58/PDF62): informal, not Lean-checked.** The owner calls identifies a slip for generates. The source introduction defines identification technically as eventual correct language-index guesses. The following finite-positive-data obstruction supports the correction independently of that comment.

Let O_odd be the positive odd numbers, E range over finite sets of positive even numbers, L_E=O_odd∪E, and K=N. Let X={K}∪{L_E:E finite}. X is countable; every candidate is infinite and every L_E is a proper subset of K. Each L_E is isolated by U_(L_E,E), while every finite F⊆K is contained in the proper candidate L_(F∩even). Thus the source containment topology has X^(1)={K}, X^(2)=∅, also under the Lean internal convention. K has no finite positive tell-tale excluding all proper subtargets.

To see the obstruction directly for an arbitrary finite-history identifier knowing the whole class, start from any finite observed prefix S. Choose E containing its even elements. Extend S along a complete enumeration of L_E until the identifier guesses L_E, which must happen if it identifies every L_E. Append a fresh even outside E, and repeat, also appending the least still-unseen natural at each stage. All finite extensions can avoid repetitions. The resulting enumeration covers K, but the identifier guesses a proper L_E at arbitrarily late stages, contradicting eventual identification of K. This uses no computability bound and gives no oracle for membership in the hidden K; it applies to the source positive-enumeration information model even with the class description known.

Embed n as (n,0) in Z^2 (or append zero coordinates). Inclusion and the finite-containment topology are unchanged, so the same countable rank-two class satisfies the higher-dimensional premises. For a fixed nondegenerate cube shape, centered dilations contain Θ(r) target points for K and each L_E, so the log-squared filter is nonvacuous. The obstruction is therefore not an empty-filter or finite-language loophole.
