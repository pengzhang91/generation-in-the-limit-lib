# Stage 2 — independent source comparison

Use the completed, frozen Stage 1 and the exact Lean snapshot and pinned paper edition named by the coordinator. Record source date, PDF hash, printed and PDF page numbers. Never substitute another version. Read the full relevant source context, including introductory conventions, definitions, remarks and interpretive proof text. Visually verify ambiguous formulas against the PDF.

First inventory the scoped source claims independently, then consult Stage 1 as a fallible intermediary. Recheck decisive Lean types and defining bodies directly. Do not use Lean comments, PaperMap, registry explanations, correction notes, old audit verdicts, theorem names, proof bodies or compilation to establish meaning. Do not silently repair the source. Record suspected source mistakes as questions for Stage 3; a difference is not by itself a Lean bug.

Map source to Lean and Lean to source, splitting multipart claims. For every unit report exact source location, Lean anchors, hypotheses, conclusions, quantifier order and witness dependencies, access/computation model, representation, distributions/losses, rates and boundary cases. Classify correspondence as exact/equivalent, faithful specialization, faithful generalization, materially different, not represented, or indeterminate. Separately assess strength, explaining opposing changes rather than forcing a total ordering. Do not infer effective procedures from arbitrary functions, uniformity from pointwise decidability, or a named witness from an unnamed existential.

Output `source-comparison.md`: identity and scope, independent source inventory, full comparison matrix, target dossiers, discrepancies, suspected source issues, Stage 1 corrections, coverage checklist and unresolved questions. Output `stage2-run.json` with hashes, actual inputs and order of access, configuration/isolation provenance and limitations. Report statements only; do not certify proof steps or human review. The coordinator verifies and freezes these outputs before developer explanations are opened for Stage 3.
