# Rule cards

Generated by `cards.py` from the grammar and the saved rule usage of the checked texts; do not edit. Each card shows the English and the Lean of one rule on placeholder arguments (P, Q: claims; X, Y: terms; T: a type; x, y, t: variables; …: the rest of the text). To review a rule, check that a mathematician reads the English as the Lean says, then put your name in the `reviewed by` column of `RuleTiers.tsv`. A reading is reviewed when every rule it uses is signed. Labels of Lean constants are made by one fixed rule (`label_words` in `selector.py`) and need no card. Rows marked “e.g.” are real examples: the shortest sentences of the checked readings and glossary definitions that use the rule, with the rest of the text as “…”, from two papers where possible.
