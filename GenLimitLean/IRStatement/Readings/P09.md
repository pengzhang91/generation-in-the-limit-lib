## Theorem 3.3

Let 𝒳 be a type. Assume that 𝒳 is *nonempty*. Assume that 𝒳 is *countable*. Let ℋ be a *language class* over 𝒳. Let 𝒜 be a sequence of subsets of 𝒳. Let α ∈ ℝ.

Suppose that ℋ is *UUS*. Suppose that 𝒜 *is countable partition*. Suppose that 0 < α.

Then ℋ is *alpha representative uniformly generatable* for 𝒜 at α if and only if ℋ *has finite group closure dimension* for 𝒜 at α.

## Theorem 3.7

Let 𝒳 be a type. Let ℋ be a *language class* over 𝒳. Let 𝒜 be a sequence of subsets of 𝒳.

The following are equivalent:
- ℋ is *representatively nonuniformly generatable* for 𝒜.
- Let α ∈ ℝ. Suppose that 0 < α. Then there is a sequence classes of *language classes* over 𝒳 with the following properties:
  - classes is *monotone* and ℋ = *i union*((n ↦ classes(n))).
  - classes(n) is *alpha representative uniformly generatable* for 𝒜 at α for every n ∈ ℕ.
