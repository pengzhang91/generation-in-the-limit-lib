## Theorem 1

Let α be a type. Assume that equality on α is decidable. Let 𝒞 be a sequence of *languages* over α.

Suppose that for every i ∈ ℕ, there is a sequence x of elements of α such that the set of values of x is exactly 𝒞(i).

Let k ∈ ℕ.

Then 𝒞 is *list identifiable* with list size k if and only if 𝒞 satisfies *k angluin condition* for k.

## Theorem 3

Let α be a type. Assume that α is *countable*. Assume that equality on α is decidable. Let 𝒞 be a sequence of *languages* over α. Let k ∈ ℕ.

Suppose that 𝒞(i) is *nonempty* for every i ∈ ℕ.

All of the following hold:
- Suppose that 𝒞 is *list identifiable* with list size k. Then there is a *kernel* 𝒜 over α and k such that 𝒜 identifies 𝒞 at *exponential* rate.
- Suppose that 𝒞 is *nontrivial for* k. Let 𝒜 be a *kernel* over α and k. There are i ∈ ℕ and a *PMF* p over α with the following property. *support*(p) = 𝒞(i) and *exp*(−2 · ↑t) ≤ *error*(𝒞, 𝒜, p, 𝒞(i), t) for infinitely many t ∈ ℕ.

## Theorem 9

Let α be a type. Let 𝒞 be a sequence of *languages* over α. Let k ∈ ℕ. Let p ∈ ℝ with p ≤ 1.

All of the following hold:
- Suppose that 𝒞 is *list identifiable* with list size k. Then *probabilistic identifies* 𝒞 with list size k with probability at least p.
- Suppose that ↑k / (↑k + 1) < p. Suppose that *probabilistic identifies* 𝒞 with list size k with probability at least p. Then 𝒞 is *list identifiable* with list size k.

## Theorem 10

Let α be a type. Assume that α is *measurable space*. Assume that α is *countable*. Assume that α is *measurable singleton class*. Let 𝒞 be a sequence of *languages* over α. Let k ∈ ℕ. Let p ∈ ℝ with p ≤ 1.

Then *infinite draws identifies* 𝒞 with list size k with probability at least p if and only if *probabilistic identifies* 𝒞 with list size k with probability at least p.
