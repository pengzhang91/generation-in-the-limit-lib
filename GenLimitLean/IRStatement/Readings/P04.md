## Theorem 1

Let α be a type. Assume that α is *infinite*. Assume that α is *countable*. Let 𝒞 be a sequence of *languages* over α.

Suppose that L is *set infinite* for every L ∈ *range*(𝒞).

Then 𝒞 is *nonuniformly generatable*.

## Theorem 3

There is a set 𝒞 of *languages* over (List Bool) with the following properties:
- 𝒞 is *set countable*.
- L is *set infinite* for every L ∈ 𝒞.
- K *is regular* for every K ∈ 𝒞.
- 𝒞 is not *exhaustively generatable*.
