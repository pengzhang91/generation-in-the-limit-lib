## Theorem 7.1

Let I, O and N be types. Assume that I is *countable*. Assume that O is *countable*. Assume that O is *nonempty*. Let naming be a *naming* over N and O. Let allowed be an *allowable* over I and O.

All of the following hold:
- Suppose that *abstract identifiable*(naming, allowed). Then allowed is *distinguishable*.
- Suppose that allowed is *collapsing uncertainty*. Let e be a sequence of elements of O. Suppose that e is *surjective*. Then *identifies*(naming, allowed, *identification by enumeration*(naming, allowed, e)).
- Suppose that allowed(o) is *set countable* for every element o of O. Suppose that allowed is *distinguishable*. Then *abstract identifiable*(naming, allowed).
