## Theorem 3.15

Let 𝓛 be an indexed family of languages L_0, L_1, L_2, … ⊆ ℕ. Each L_i is infinite. 𝓛 has a membership oracle. Let x be a sequence of natural numbers. Let z ∈ ℕ.

Suppose that the set of values of x is exactly L_z.

Then 1 / 2 ≤ *patient lower density*(𝓛, x, z).

## Theorem B.3

Let 𝓛 be an indexed family of languages L_0, L_1, L_2, … ⊆ ℕ. Each L_i is infinite. 𝓛 has a membership oracle. Let x be a sequence of natural numbers. Let K' be a *language*. Let z ∈ ℕ.

Suppose that the set of values of x is exactly K'. Suppose that K' is *set infinite*. Suppose that K' ⊆ L_z.

Let α ∈ ℝ.

Suppose that *relative lower density*(K', L_z, *oracle family infinite*(𝓛, z)) = α.

Then α / 2 ≤ *partial patient lower density*(𝓛, x, z).
