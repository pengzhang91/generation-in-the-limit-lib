## Theorem 3.1

Let 𝒳 be a type. Assume that 𝒳 is *countable*. Assume that 𝒳 is *infinite*. Let ℋ be a *language class* over 𝒳.

Suppose that ℋ is *UUS*.

Then ℋ is *uniform noise independent generatable* if and only if *common intersection*(ℋ) is *set infinite*.

## Theorem 3.3

Let 𝒳 be a type. Assume that 𝒳 is *countable*. Assume that 𝒳 is *nonempty*. Let ℋ be a *language class* over 𝒳.

Suppose that ℋ is *UUS*.

The following are equivalent:
- ℋ is *uniform noise dependent generatable*.
- Let n ∈ ℕ. Then ℋ has *finite noisy closure dimension at* noise level n.

## Theorem 3.10

Let 𝒳 be a type. Assume that 𝒳 is *countable*. Assume that 𝒳 is *infinite*. Let ℋ be a *language class* over 𝒳.

Suppose that ℋ is *UUS*. Suppose that there is k ∈ ℕ such that there is a function classes from {0, …, k − 1} to *language class*(𝒳) such that ℋ = *i union*((i ↦ classes(i))) and classes(i) is *uniform noise independent generatable* for every i ∈ {0, …, k − 1}.

Then ℋ is *noisily generatable in limit*.
