## *online learnable*

Let α be a type. Let H be a *language class* over α.

By definition, H is *online learnable* if and only if the following holds.

There are an *online predictor* A over α and a sequence R of natural numbers with the following properties:
- Let k ∈ ℕ. There is T ∈ ℕ, depending only on α, H, A, R and k, with the following property. Let t ∈ ℕ. Suppose that t ≥ T. Then k · R(t) ≤ t.
- Let K ∈ H. Let xs be a list of pairs in α × Bool. Suppose that *labeled by*(K, xs). Then *mistakes from*(A, [], xs) ≤ R(*length*(xs)).
