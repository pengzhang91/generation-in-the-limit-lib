## *achieves noisy time vector*

Let α be a type. Let G be a *history generator* over α. Let F be a sequence of subsets of α. Let noise be a sequence of natural numbers. Let time be a *time vector*.

By definition, G *achieves noisy time vector* time on F at noise levels noise if and only if the following holds.

Let coordinate and t be natural numbers. Let xs be a function from {0, …, t − 1} to α.

Suppose that time(coordinate) ≤ t. Suppose that xs is injective. Suppose that *noise contained at*(F, noise, coordinate, *sequence sample*(xs)).

All of the following hold:
- G(t, xs) ∈ F(coordinate).
- G(t, xs) ≠ xs(k) for every k ∈ {0, …, t − 1}.

## *achieves time vector*

Let α be a type. Let G be a *history generator* over α. Let F be a sequence of subsets of α. Let time be a *time vector*.

By definition, G *achieves time vector* time on F if and only if the following holds.

Let i and t be natural numbers. Let xs be a function from {0, …, t − 1} to α.

Suppose that time(i) ≤ t. Suppose that xs is injective. Suppose that xs(k) ∈ F(i) for every k ∈ {0, …, t − 1}.

- G(t, xs) ∈ F(i).
- G(t, xs) ≠ xs(k) for every k ∈ {0, …, t − 1}.

## *pareto optimal*

Let Achievable be a set of *time vectors*. Let t be a *time vector*.

By definition, t is *pareto optimal* in Achievable if and only if the following holds.

Let s ∈ Achievable.

Suppose that *improves somewhere*(s, t).

Then *worse somewhere*(s, t).

By definition, *realizable*(F, groups, ε) = (the set of *time vectors* t such that t is *positive time vector* and there is a function G from words over α to *discrete distribution*(α) such that for every word xs over α, if *to finset*(xs) is *finset nonempty*, then *group sup distance*(G(xs), *to finset*(xs), groups) ≤ *of real*(ε) and for every i ∈ ℕ, if |*to finset*(xs)| ≥ t(i), then if *to finset*(xs) ⊆ F(i), then *supported on*(G(xs), F(i) ∖ *to finset*(xs))).
