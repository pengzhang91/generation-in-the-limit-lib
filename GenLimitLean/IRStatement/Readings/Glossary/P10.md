## *eventually unbounded closure*

Let α be a type. Let H be a *language class* over α.

By definition, H is *eventually unbounded closure* if and only if the following holds.

Let L ∈ H. Let x be a *stream* over α.

Suppose that *presents*(x, L).

Then there is t ∈ ℕ such that *common core*(H, {x(n) : n < t}) is *infinite*.
