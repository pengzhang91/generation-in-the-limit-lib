## *differentially private*

Let ι, X and Y be types. Assume that Y is *measurable space*. Let ε ∈ ℝ. Let δ be an *ENN real*. Let law be a function from (ι → X) to *measure*(Y).

By definition, law is *differentially private* at ε with slack δ if and only if the following holds.

Let u and v be functions from ι to X.

Suppose that *neighbors*(u, v).

Let E ⊆ Y.

Suppose that E is *measurable set*.

Then *coe*(law(u), E) ≤ (*of real*(*exp*(ε)) · *coe*(law(v), E)) + δ.
