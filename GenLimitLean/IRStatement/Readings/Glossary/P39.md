## *partial patient lower density*

Let O be an indexed family of languages L_0, L_1, L_2, … ⊆ ℕ. Each L_i is infinite. O has a membership oracle. Let x be a sequence of natural numbers. Let z ∈ ℕ.

By definition, *partial patient lower density*(O, x, z) = *relative lower density*(*generator first*(x, *output*(*closure*(O), x)) ∩ L_z, L_z, *oracle family infinite*(O, z)).

[Lean definition](../../../GenLimit/Paper39_DenseGeneration/Partial/Main.lean#L21) · `GenLimit.PartialEnumeration.partialPatientLowerDensity`
